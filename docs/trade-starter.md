# Starter profiles

New profiles start in **Compare multiple rewards** mode with the Trade / mapping
starter. Existing saved profiles keep their mode and weights.

| Reward | Kind | Points |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Exceptional Gem | Combo | 10 |
| Item Rarity | Combo | 4 |
| Dreadmaidens' Sacrament | Combo | 2 |
| Additional Currency Items | Regular | 3 |
| Additional Currency Items | Combo | 4 |

| Highly Increased Item Rarity | Regular | 2 |

| Additional Threads | Regular | 1 |

The weights are provisional preferences, independent of prices or expected profit.
A regular reward scores per confirmed icon; a combo scores once when completed.
Similar rewards remain separate: family headings do not combine their counters.
Shared orange-feather icons need exact tooltip confirmation before scoring.

Open **Reward priorities → Starter priorities** to choose:

- **Trade / mapping**: the 12 priorities above.
- **Campaign / SSF**: regular Currency 3, combo Currency 4, Gem with Quality 2,
  Additional Gems Some with Maximum Quality combo 4, Rare Boots with a Speed
  Modifier 3, Rare Weapons with a Physical Modifier 2, and Rare Map 2.
- **Viscera rewards**: Viscera Item combo 10. This values the explicit reward;
  it does not estimate boss drop chances or infer bonuses from duplicates.

**Replace priorities** shows the current and replacement counts, then replaces
all priorities while preserving filters and personal templates. **Use trade
starter** instead updates or adds only the 12 trade rewards, keeping other goals.
If that would exceed 30 priorities, remove enough goals first.

**Unweighted rewards** beside advice lists identified, eligible reward types with
no positive weight. They add no points, but are not assumed to have no value.
The panel opens the existing priority editor. Reward definitions in the reference
search do not become goals or confirm observations automatically.
