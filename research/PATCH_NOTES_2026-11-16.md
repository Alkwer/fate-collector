  of one Fate are allowed; first and third rewards are tripled, second ignored.
  Both planners now consider the three choices of middle card. Regular rewards
  score ×3 on both retained cards. Equal known, reviewed tiers are a conservative
  application boundary; the note does not describe mixed-tier behavior. No output
  tier, combo multiplier, repeated payout or additional input slot is inferred.

  no longer grants Culling Strike; Crowkeeper explosions and Wildfire walls deal
  less damage. Some Fate-spawned bosses can now drop Viscera correctly. The notes
  give no numerical damage reduction or Viscera probability. Existing screenshot
  transcriptions are historical evidence and are not rewritten as current tooltips.
- [Work in progress](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/4015561): new
  Fate reward outcomes and icon-size changes remain announcements in the inspected
  patch set. No new reward definitions, recognition templates or trade weights added.
